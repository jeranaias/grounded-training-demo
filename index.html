Trigger control and follow-through minimize your arc of movement so the bullet leaves the muzzle while the sights are still on target. It requires a smooth, consistent rearward squeeze, with the finger where it naturally lays for the best mechanical advantage. The most common error is dropping control after the shot — keep your head on the stock, eye open, and hold the trigger to the rear through recoil until you feel the sear reset.
