What is sight alignment?
Sight alignment is the relationship between the rear aperture, the front sight post, and the aiming eye — the post centered and level in the aperture.
TC 3-22.9, Ch 7 · p.7-3
⛔ Refused

Generated micro-lesson · trigger control
A smooth, consistent rearward squeeze so the round leaves the muzzle while the sights are still on target. The most common error is dropping control after the shot — hold the trigger to the rear through recoil until the sear resets.
